ColorSand privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

ColorSand is a puzzle game for Android published by Felix Dombek.

What we collect

Nothing. ColorSand does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not use the internet.

Data on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google account backup. We never see it.

Children

ColorSand does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that change this, such as ads or online features, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
